3. User Interaction
    1. Potential Users: Those interested in working out who don't know where to start or can't afford a personal trainer.
    2. Interact: Using a drop down menu, they could sort by  muscle group, equipment available, and the website would show them a list of exercises that meet their specifications.
    3. Potential Benefits - Our data could help users who otherwise couldn't get started in the gym by providing them with exercises to do and showing them how to do them. Additionally, it could be used by more experienced lifters looking for different exercise ideas.
    4. Potential Harms - It is possible for beginners to get injured when doing exercises, especially those that require gym equipment. Furthermore, some of the exercises may not be possible for those with limited equipment, or for those with disabilities. We mitigate that by providing a wide range of exercises for all equipment and body types.
